Clarifications

Service FAQ

Does applying affect my credit score?

No. Our initial application and pre-approval process uses a soft credit pull, meaning your FICO score remains completely untouched while you review your offers.

How fast can I get equipment funding?

In many cases, equipment financing can be approved in 2-4 hours and funded directly to the vendor within 24 to 48 hours, allowing you to pick up the machinery instantly.

Do I need collateral for working capital?

Generally, no. Working capital loans and lines of credit up to certain limits are unsecured, based primarily on your business's monthly revenue and cash flow history.
