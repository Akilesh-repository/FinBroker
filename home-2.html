Need to Know

Do you finance startups?

Yes, however, most startup funding requires strong personal credit (700+) from the owners or substantial collateral.

What is the minimum credit score required?

We have programs for credit scores as low as 500, provided your business demonstrates strong, consistent monthly revenue.

Are there early payoff penalties?

Many of our lending partners offer early payoff discounts or have zero pre-payment penalties. Your advisor will clarify this per offer.
