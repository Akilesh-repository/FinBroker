About Our Organization

Driving Business Growth Through Strategic Capital.

FinBroker was founded on a simple premise: acquiring the right equipment and capital shouldn't be the hardest part of running a business. We act as your dedicated financial advocate.

By leveraging our extensive network of over 75 premium lenders, we bypass the rigid constraints of traditional banking. Whether you are a startup needing your first truck or an enterprise scaling manufacturing, we tailor the exact financial instrument for your success.

Our Journey

A History of Growth

A straightforward look at how we grew from a small local brokerage to a national financing powerhouse.

2015

The Foundation

Founded in a small NY office with a core team of three, focusing entirely on local heavy equipment leasing.

2018

Network Expansion

Expanded our lender network to over 30 alternative banks, introducing working capital and SBA loans.

2021

Digital Evolution

Launched our proprietary digital portal, reducing average funding time from 7 days to just 24 hours.

2026

National Leader

Surpassed $500M in total funded capital, serving thousands of businesses across all 50 states.
